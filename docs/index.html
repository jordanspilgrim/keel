⎈ Keel

The retention flywheel

An AI agent that saves cancellations, grades itself on every conversation, and tells the product team what to build next — safe and EU-AI-Act-compliant by design.

Proof of concept  Python + OpenAI  Runs on a laptop  Synthetic data

The idea in one minute

A customer opens a chat to cancel. An AI agent tries to keep them — but only with offers that policy actually allows. Every one of those conversations is then graded by a second AI, and the graded conversations are clustered into themes that tell you why people are really leaving and which offers keep them without giving away the margin.

The output of the last step feeds the first: new offers and policies go back to the agent, and the grader tells you whether they helped. That closed loop — not the chatbot — is the whole point.

Most “AI agent” demos stop at the agent. Keel builds the loop around it: act, measure, learn — then act again on what it learned.

Three layers, one loop

Act

Cancellation-saver

Runs the live retention conversation. Detects churn intent, picks a policy-compliant offer, records the outcome — saved, lost, or escalated.

Measure

Eval harness

Grades every conversation — resolution, policy, offer fit, tone, hallucination, plus a fairness slice — with a golden set to catch regressions.

Learn

Voice-of-customer

Clusters graded conversations into churn themes and ranked roadmap signals: why they churn, which offers retain, at what margin cost.

Safe & compliant by design

The same observability spine that makes Keel measurable also makes it safe. Guardrails sit around the agent as enforced layers it can’t talk its way past, and the EU compliance pieces are built in, not bolted on:

Told it’s an AI (EU AI Act Art. 50) · a human confirms consequential actions (GDPR Art. 22) · PII scrubbed before anything is logged or embedded · a fairness slice in every grade · a full audit trail. On the seeded red-team, the guardrails caught 100% of jailbreak, off-scope, and PII attempts.
